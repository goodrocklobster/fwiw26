About the data & calculations

A small, deliberate selection

These 48 items were selected from the supplied 2026 commissary list to show everyday hygiene, health, food, correspondence, clothing and household needs, alongside occasional purchases. This is an illustrative selection, not a statistical sample, a recommended shopping list, or a claim about what DOC provides. Categories are editorial groupings. Prices and product names are unchanged.

The starting basket contains one Coast Soap, one Tek Excel Toothbrush, and one Stamped Envelope. Quantities are editable. No replenishment schedule or purchase limits are assumed or enforced. Source markers such as ** and -K are retained; their definitions were not supplied.

Source

FF26- Data Model Template v2.xlsx: selected rows from Costs!A2:C475; Average_Hourly from Wages!E2:E5. All figures are a snapshot of the supplied model. The $16.94 minimum-wage benchmark has not been independently verified.

What the numbers mean

Basket cost = listed price × quantity, summed across items. Paid hours = basket cost ÷ hourly wage. The time multiplier = minimum wage ÷ DOC wage.

The equivalent price = basket cost × time multiplier. It is the hypothetical price a minimum-wage worker would need to pay to spend the same paid work hours on that basket. It is not an observed retail price outside prison.

DOC Tier 1: $0.09/hour; Tier 2: $0.60/hour; Tier 3: $1.60/hour. These are the supplied averages, not range midpoints. Calculations assume all gross pay is available, with no deductions, other expenses, or transfers. Paid hours do not establish work availability or elapsed time. No workday estimate is shown without a documented schedule. Displayed results are rounded; calculations use unrounded rates.
